Inspired by the rape trial in Cork, November 2018, where a 17-year old's lace pants were used by defense as an indication of her consent, The Noisy Movement (TNM) was created to make noise about topical issues in our communities. We are a group of students and friends based in Edinburgh, who aim to raise awareness and funds for groups in society that need our support. We are currently running two activist campaigns: ThisIsNotConsent & MindTheNoise.
