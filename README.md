# the-noisy-movement
The Noisy Movement (TNM) Website: https://the-noisy-movement.github.io/the-noisy-movement/          

Inspired by the rape trial in Cork in November 2018, where a 17-year-old's lace pants were used by the defence as an indication of her consent, The Noisy Movement was created to make noise about topical issues in our communities. We are a group of students and friends based in Edinburgh who aim to raise awareness and funds for groups in society that need our support. We are currently running two activist campaigns: ThisIsNotConsent & MindTheNoise.        

We are a student-led nonprofit social enterprise that aims to make noise and raise awareness for important causes that support vulnerable groups of our society.
